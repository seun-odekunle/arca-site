Privacy

Last updated October 15, 2026

This page covers arcaholdingsllc.com and email sent to Arca. Each app has its own privacy policy. Ledger’s is at keepingledger.com/privacy.

The short version

  • This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics, ads or tracking.
  • It doesn’t ask you for anything. There are no forms and no accounts.
  • If you email Arca, your message is used only to reply.

This website

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub’s Privacy Statement. Arca doesn’t receive those records.

The site’s fonts and images are served from the site itself, so loading a page contacts no other company.

Email

Email to Arca is handled by Apple’s iCloud Mail. Arca keeps messages only as long as needed to reply and follow up, and deletes them on request. Your address is never added to a mailing list or shared.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask what Arca holds about you (usually just your emails) and ask for it to be deleted. Write to hello@arcaholdingsllc.com.
